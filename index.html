01 A historical misadventure

Act I / Sarajevo / 1914

Sarajevo

A teenage revolutionary, an Archduke and a string of coincidences so absurd they would be rejected by any sensible editor.

A tragicomic song-film about the morning the twentieth century began badly, and the young man who discovered that changing history is much easier than living with it.

“A most curious thing to say. As if one has the ability to control one’s own death.”
02 A family investigation

Act II / Ireland & Liverpool

Annie’s Ghost

It is most relieving to discover one descends from the criminally insane and the drunk. It explains a great deal.

Prison ledgers, Catholic records, larceny, love and one formidable woman who crossed the Irish Sea and left an indelible mark on a family tree populated largely by the unremarkable.

“Say what you will about Irish Catholics, but they kept excellent records.”

Meet the troupe

Nobody asked history to become a concept album.

Wooznam & The Lickers did it anyway.

This is a home for darkly comic songs and cinematic films made from true stories. The facts are researched. The dignity is handled less carefully.

Here you will find revolutionaries, clowns, criminals, ghosts and ordinary people who wandered into history before history had the decency to warn them.
