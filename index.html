About Us

Wooznam

Who is Wooznam?

Nobody knows. More significantly, nobody generally cares.

But they should.

Wooznam is part human. Almost certainly part machine.

He writes. Sometimes sings.

Wherever there is tragedy, political violence, family dysfunction or an interesting death, Wooznam is sniffing about.

Not helping, obviously.

Just watching.
